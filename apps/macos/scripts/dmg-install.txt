Installing Zephyr
=================

1. Drag Zephyr.app onto the Applications folder.

2. Sign it on your Mac (once per download).

   Zephyr is open source and is not notarized by Apple: the project has no
   paid Apple Developer ID, so the app in this disk image carries only an
   ad-hoc signature and macOS refuses to open the downloaded copy. Open
   Terminal and run:

     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Zephyr.app
     codesign --force --deep --preserve-metadata=entitlements,flags --sign - /Applications/Zephyr.app

   The first command removes the "downloaded from the internet" mark; the
   second signs the app ad-hoc on your own Mac, keeping the entitlements of
   the Quick Look extension.

3. Open Zephyr from Applications.

Requirements: macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

Finder preview (Space bar): Zephyr > Settings > General > Documents.
Source code, issues and license texts: https://github.com/W-ZN/Zephyr
