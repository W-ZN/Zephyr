Zephyr 0.1.4

  • Find and Replace in the document: a Zephyr bar (⌘F, ⌥⌘F) with Match Case, Whole Word and Regular Expression, matches highlighted on the page.
  • Folder search ranks results by relatedness (word forms, Chinese character pairs, nearby words), computed on demand: no index, nothing kept in memory.
  • Animated GIFs play in place while visible, at most two at a time; Settings → General → Animated GIFs, Reduce Motion and Low Power Mode keep them still.
  • Export can use light, dark or the system appearance (export panel, Settings → Export, --appearance).
