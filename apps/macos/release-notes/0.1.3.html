Zephyr 0.1.3

  • Bold, italic and strikethrough now render when a closing mark sits between punctuation and text, as in **差异A：**内容 or **Note:**text; the same marks export correctly.
  • Typing * adds its partner everywhere: at the start of a line (a space inside the pair makes a bullet) and beside Chinese, Japanese or Korean text without a space. Up to three nested pairs.
